About This Book

Massé Life is a gripping psychological thriller that explores the dangerous intersection of love, trauma, and redemption. Set against the backdrop of professional billiards, this dark romantic suspense novel follows characters who must confront their deepest wounds while navigating a deadly game where every shot could be their last.

Vietnamese author Ung Hoang Phi Dang masterfully weaves themes of healing, betrayal, and dangerous attraction into a story that will keep you on the edge of your seat. When the path you choose to walk is blocked, you must learn to change your trajectory in unexpected ways—just like executing the perfect Massé shot.
